About GreenLeaf Organics

A Sydney family business rooted in a love for good food and a healthy planet.

Our Story

GreenLeaf Organics was founded in 2018 by Emma and James Chen, two passionate foodies who were frustrated by the lack of truly fresh, certified organic produce available in Sydney supermarkets.

What started as a small weekend market stall in Newtown quickly grew into a full delivery service, connecting hundreds of Sydney households directly with the farmers who grow their food.

Today we work with over 20 certified organic farms across New South Wales, delivering weekly boxes of seasonal produce to more than 2,000 families across Greater Sydney.

Our Mission

To make certified organic, locally grown food accessible and affordable for every Sydney family — while supporting the farmers and ecosystems that make it possible.

Our Core Values

The principles that guide everything we do.

🌿

Sustainability

We minimise waste, use compostable packaging, and only partner with farms that practise regenerative agriculture.

🤝

Community

We pay farmers fairly, support local food banks, and invest in the communities that feed us.

✨

Quality

Every item is hand-selected and quality-checked before it reaches your door. No compromises.

💚

Integrity

We are transparent about our sourcing, pricing, and practices. What you see is what you get.
